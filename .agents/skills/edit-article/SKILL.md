---
name: edit-article
description:
  "Edit or review a technical article for accurate claims, usable examples, and
  clear explanations."
---

Establish the intended audience and whether the request is for critique, a
rewrite, or both. Preserve the author's purpose and voice while correcting
technical errors and misleading explanations.

Check consequential claims against supplied evidence, code, versioned behavior,
or authoritative sources. Verify examples when practical and safe. Distinguish
measured results, general claims, and assumptions; do not invent citations,
benchmarks, or certainty.

Check that readers have the prerequisites to follow the examples, that defaults
and limitations are clear, and that failure cases materially affecting the
advice are covered. Bring the main lesson forward. Cut repetition and jargon
when it impedes understanding; do not expand every article into an exhaustive
manual.

For critique, lead with publish / revise / reject and the main reason.
Prioritize specific findings with a section or line, the incorrect or unclear
claim, its consequence, and a suggested correction. Separate blocking
inaccuracies from optional editorial improvements. No finding quota or forced
skepticism.

For an edit request, deliver or apply the revised article, then summarize
material corrections and unresolved fact checks. Do not stop at reviewing an
article the user asked you to rewrite. Editing does not authorize publishing it.
